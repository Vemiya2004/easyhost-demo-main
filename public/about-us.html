What is EasyH?

Creating Beautiful Map Portraits, Instantly

EasyH is a free online tool that generates stunning, stylized map portraits of any city in the world. Simply choose a location, upload a photo of yourself, and we'll create a beautiful composite artwork that combines your portrait with the map of that special place — all completely free, no sign-up required.

Whether you want to commemorate a favorite travel destination, celebrate where you're from, or create a meaningful gift for someone special, EasyH makes it simple. In just three steps, you'll have a high-quality, shareable artwork ready to download and enjoy.

Our technology uses OpenStreetMap data to accurately render the streets and layout of your chosen city, combined with your personal photo to create a unique, one-of-a-kind piece of art that tells your story.

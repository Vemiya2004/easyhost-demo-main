Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about EasyH, map portraits, and how to create beautiful artwork.

Getting Started
A map portrait is a unique artistic creation that combines your personal photograph with a stylized map of a meaningful location. We blend your photo with the street layout and features of your chosen city to create a beautiful, one-of-a-kind piece of art that tells your story.
Creating a portrait is simple! Visit the main EasyH website, then: (1) Choose any city in the world, (2) Upload a photo of yourself, (3) Customize the map style and colors, (4) Download your high-resolution portrait. The entire process takes just a few minutes and requires no sign-up.
No! EasyH is completely free and requires zero sign-up. You don't need an account, email confirmation, or any personal registration. Just visit, create, and download. We designed it this way to respect your privacy and your time.
Yes, 100% free! There are no hidden fees, no premium tiers, and no paid upgrades. You can create unlimited map portraits at no cost. We offer EasyH free to everyone because we believe personalized artwork should be accessible to all.
Customization
You can create map portraits for any city in the world with available map data. We use OpenStreetMap, which covers hundreds of thousands of locations globally. Simply search for your city name—we'll find it instantly. If your exact location isn't available, try nearby cities or regions.
Absolutely! EasyH offers multiple map styles and color schemes for customization. You can choose different visual themes to match your personal taste or home decor. The goal is to create artwork that's uniquely yours.
Of course! Since creation is instant and free, you can create as many variations as you'd like. Adjust the map style, colors, layout, or try a different photo. Regenerate until you're completely satisfied with the result.
Any clear photo of yourself will work! Portrait photos with good lighting are ideal. You can optionally remove the background for a cleaner result, or keep the background if you prefer. The more recognizable you are in the photo, the better the final portrait will look.
Technical Questions
You can upload JPG, PNG, or GIF photos. The generated portrait is exported as a high-quality PNG image at 900×1200px resolution, which is perfect for digital sharing or professional printing.
Most portraits are generated instantly or within 30 seconds. Processing time depends on file size and current server load, but we've optimized our system for speed. You'll see your finished portrait almost immediately after uploading your photo.
Every portrait is generated at 900×1200px resolution in PNG format. This size is ideal for social media sharing, printing at standard 8x10" or 11x14" sizes, or displaying on digital screens. It's large enough for high-quality prints.
No, we don't store your photos. Your image is processed in real-time and immediately discarded after your portrait is generated. We never retain your personal photos, never share them with third parties, and never use them for any purpose other than creating your portrait.
Yes. Privacy is core to EasyH. We don't collect personal information, track users, or share data with anyone. Since you don't need to sign up, we don't even know who you are. Your photos are processed locally and never stored on our servers.
